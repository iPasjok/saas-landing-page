Questions, answered

Does my client need an account?

No. They open a link, review the invoice and pay by card or bank transfer. That's it.

Which currencies and countries do you support?

You can invoice in 30+ currencies, and tax lines can be set per invoice so they match your local rules.

How do the reminders work?

You choose the schedule, for example three days before the due date and seven days after. Each email is short, friendly and sent from your name.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Paid plans have no contract. If you cancel, you drop back to the free plan and keep all your invoices.
